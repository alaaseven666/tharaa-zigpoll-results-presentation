THE KEY INSIGHT

“Too expensive” often means “I'm not yet convinced it's worth it”

SURVEY 1 FINDING

17.3% leave because there isn't enough product info or usage guidance on the page.

→
SURVEY 2 FINDING

The same three topics — usage, results timeline, and purity — make up 63% of what shoppers say they need before buying.

→
WHAT IT MEANS

Price objections are inflated by an information gap. Closing that gap is cheaper and faster than any pricing change.

06 / 08
RECOMMENDATIONS · PART 1

Quick wins (low effort, high impact)

01

Rebuild product pages around 4 questions

Add clear “How to use,” “What it treats,” “When you'll see results,” and “Purity / sourcing” sections to every product — directly answers 87% of Survey 2.

02

Introduce a visible entry-level offer

A first-order discount, bundle, or buy-2-get-1 promo shown before exit — requested repeatedly and targets the #1 exit reason (57.6%).

03

Add “shop by concern” navigation

Let shoppers filter by skin/hair concern instead of product name — addresses the 20.7% who couldn't find what they wanted.

04

Show live stock status + restock alerts

Rose oil, rosehip oil and tea-tree oil were named repeatedly as sold out — a “notify me” button recaptures that demand.

07 / 08
RECOMMENDATIONS · PART 2

Medium-term moves (build on the momentum)

05

Launch a “find your routine” helper

A short quiz or WhatsApp consultation for shoppers who explicitly asked someone to recommend the right products for them.

06

Offer trial / travel sizes

Smaller sizes at accessible price points soften the “expensive for so little” friction without discounting full-size margin.

07

Publish certifications & sourcing

Lab-test results and an ingredient-sourcing story build the trust that a market full of counterfeit “natural” products has eroded.

08

Set and advertise a free-shipping threshold

Shipping cost and unclear delivery terms came up repeatedly, especially for orders outside Cairo and international orders.
